Vaulture is built as a complete end-to-end form platform, similar in usability to Google Forms, but designed with stronger branding, premium customization, admin-level control, and production-grade security. From form creation to response collection, user notifications, payments, and moderation, every part of the system is designed to be reliable, scalable, and secure.

The platform allows users to create fully dynamic forms through an intuitive interface, making it easy to launch professional forms without technical complexity. Whether it is a simple feedback form or a more advanced structured workflow, Vaulture adapts seamlessly to different use cases.

Vaulture includes a powerful super admin panel used by the head team of the application to monitor all forms created by users across the platform. If a form receives a high number of user flags or suspicious activity, the admin can review and deactivate it to maintain platform trust, quality, and safety.




Premium users get access to advanced branding features, including up to two custom subdomains of their choice. By default, forms are served through a secure subdomain structure such as secure.domain.com, giving users a professional and trusted experience while maintaining deployment consistency across the platform.




The system also includes integrated payment services with three different plans, enabling a clean upgrade path for users based on their needs. Each plan is structured to support different levels of usage, customization, and premium benefits, making the platform suitable for both individuals and growing teams.




On the technical side, Vaulture is engineered as a complete service ecosystem. It includes rate limiting to prevent abuse, automated email services to notify users whenever a new form response is submitted, dynamic response handling, and a scalable backend architecture built to support continuous growth and reliable performance.




Security is one of the strongest foundations of Vaulture. The platform is protected using Cloudflare services for performance optimization, traffic filtering, and enhanced security at the edge. Combined with backend validation, rate limiting, secure payment handling, and controlled admin moderation, the system is designed to deliver a dependable and technically robust form-building experience.
