Graphic Designer

What I worked on

Visual assets for fast-moving brand communication.

At Depixed Media, I worked on social media creatives, branding assets, and visual design deliverables that needed to communicate clearly and look polished across different formats. The role trained me to think in composition, hierarchy, contrast, and audience attention.

Problems I solved

Making ideas clear at a glance.

Design work taught me to simplify complexity: choose the strongest message, structure the layout around it, and remove visual noise. That same instinct now helps me build dashboards and interfaces where users can scan, decide, and act quickly.

Hierarchy

Organized information so the most important message became easy to see first.

Consistency

Built assets that felt coherent across different campaign and brand contexts.

Usability Sense

Developed the visual judgment I now apply to clean software product interfaces.

Impact

A design foundation that strengthened my engineering work.

This experience sits early in my career, but it still matters. It gave me a sharp eye for spacing, visual rhythm, and user perception. As a software engineer, that helps me build interfaces that are not only functional, but easier for people to understand.
